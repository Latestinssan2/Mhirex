# Mhirex 1.0-beta11

## What's new

- Add one-tap edit templates for Freeze, Noir, and Birthday videos, with original sound effects and a synthesized birthday melody.
- Add text animation presets for Typewriter, Bounce, Blur reveal, and 3D extrude, plus Bounce, Pop, Spin, and Fade sticker motion.
- Add face-tracked goggles and skull-mask overlays using on-device face landmarks.
- Add Pixabay image and video discovery with attribution, source links, and a usage-rights confirmation before importing video.
- Add saved Instagram and YouTube creator watermarks, including the service mark and channel name.
- Add automatic transitions, clip transitions, speed changes for selected time ranges, background options, voiceover, vibration feedback, and multiple export resolutions.
- Refresh the landing page and the @_fivetriple.8_ creator image, and remove the decorative orb that protruded from the onboarding card.

## Rights and audio

Use only media you own or have permission to use. Mhirex does not download or extract audio from Spotify or YouTube links; those links open their official services. Import audio files you have rights to use. Pixabay search results identify their creator and source for review.
