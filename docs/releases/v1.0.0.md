# Mhirex 1.0.0

Mhirex 1.0.0 is the first stable release. It includes the editing and reliability work delivered during the beta cycle, plus the fixes listed below.

## Editing and templates

- Death Freeze now uses the clip under the playhead without requiring a prior clip selection. The freeze is a three-second black-and-white clip with an animated shake; its skull, frame, overlays and text receive the same timeline-wide monochrome grade.
- Freeze creation handles gallery media, reports encoding failures in the app log, and retries with a compatible H.264 encoder when hardware encoding fails.
- Happy Birthday balloons rise with independent position and rotation keyframes. Template layers remain grouped on the timeline.
- Image overlays, template frames and skull art can be selected from the preview and adjusted for position, size and rotation. Image rotation keyframes also render in preview.
- The font picker has search and larger, clearer samples. Color grades include monochrome, cinematic, cartoon, and other presets; adjustments include brightness, tint, contrast, shadows, and highlights. The editor also includes background removal and customizable blur and motion blur.

## Media and projects

- Pixabay image requests retry after rate limits; audio and sound effects can be downloaded from inside the app.
- Exports are saved under `DCIM/Mhirex` and open the Android sharing panel.
- Recent projects save automatically and show an image preview.
